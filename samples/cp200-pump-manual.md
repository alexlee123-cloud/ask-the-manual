# CP-200 Centrifugal Pump Service Manual (Sample)

This is a short, made-up manual for testing Ask the Manual without downloading
real documents. The CP-200 is not a real product.

## Safety

Before any service, lock out and tag out the motor disconnect and verify zero
energy by attempting to start the pump at the local control station. Close the
suction and discharge valves and drain the casing through the drain plug at the
bottom of the volute. Wear safety glasses and nitrile gloves.

## Specifications

- Rated flow: 200 gallons per minute at 1,750 RPM
- Maximum discharge pressure: 150 psi
- Motor: 10 horsepower, 460 V, 3-phase
- Impeller diameter: 8.5 inches
- Bearing lubricant: lithium-complex NLGI grade 2 grease

## Lubrication

Grease the motor-end and pump-end bearings every 2,000 operating hours. Add
0.25 ounces of grease to each bearing (about 6 strokes of a standard grease
gun). Do not over-grease: excess grease causes the bearings to run hot. Bearing
housing temperature should stay below 180 degrees F after the first hour.

## Mechanical Seal Replacement

1. Lock out the pump and drain the casing (see Safety).
2. Remove the eight 1/2-inch casing bolts and slide the back pull-out assembly free.
3. Hold the shaft with a strap wrench and remove the impeller nut (left-hand thread).
4. Pull the impeller and remove the old seal rotating element.
5. Clean the shaft sleeve and lightly coat it with silicone grease.
6. Install the new seal by hand. Never hammer seal faces.
7. Reinstall the impeller and torque the impeller nut to 45 ft-lb.
8. Torque the casing bolts to 60 ft-lb in a star pattern.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low flow | Clogged suction strainer | Clean the strainer |
| Loud rattling, like gravel | Cavitation from low suction pressure | Open the suction valve fully; check NPSH |
| Leak at the shaft | Worn mechanical seal | Replace the seal |
| Bearings over 180 F | Over-greasing or misalignment | Purge excess grease; check alignment |

## Alarm Codes

- E01: Motor overload. Check for a locked rotor or a closed discharge valve.
- E02: High bearing temperature. Stop the pump and inspect the bearings.
- E03: Low suction pressure. Check the suction valve and strainer.
